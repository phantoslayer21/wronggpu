THIRD-PARTY NOTICES (WrongGPU 0.6.0)

WrongGPU 0.6.0 contains no third-party code. At build time it uses type declarations (structures, constants,
function signatures) from AMD's FidelityFX SDK API headers, which are MIT-licensed. Their licence texts are in
wronggpu-notices.txt, which comes with every release.

Not part of WrongGPU and not in its download:
  - OptiScaler (GPL-3.0). The launcher downloads the WrongGPU edition of OptiScaler from its own release page,
    https://github.com/phantoslayer21/OptiScaler/releases/tag/wronggpu-edition-20261102.1 (source and licence
    there), checks it against a fixed SHA-256, and puts OptiScaler's own files, its licence included, next to the
    game.
  - The game, its own AMD FidelityFX files (kept in the game folder, renamed), AMD's graphics driver, and the DLSS
    files already on the player's PC. WrongGPU converts the model on the player's own PC; the result stays there.
