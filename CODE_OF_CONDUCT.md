# Code of conduct

WrongGPU follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults,
personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not accepted, in the project's
issues, pull requests, discussions and community channels alike.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately: through the private reporting form on the **Security** tab
of https://github.com/phantoslayer21/wronggpu (it is fine to use it for a conduct report), or in a private message to a
maintainer on the WrongGPU Discord server (https://discord.gg/745z9MfKwm). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers decide on the consequences described in
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
